i woke up 
the sun was already up
then
reality hit

tears pouring down
even before
my brain can process

feeling of a void
growing fast
until nothing left inside
only emptiness, i felt

nobody here
nobody there
there is nobody
i have nobody

my 10-year companion
passed
Silver, my cat
i miss you

i now feel
what i had taken for granted
is no longer there
Silver, he passed

nobody
nothing
it's empty

that void
once was filled
by you
now, it's left empty

i woke up
from the void
then
reality hit

he is no longer there
Silver, he passed away
